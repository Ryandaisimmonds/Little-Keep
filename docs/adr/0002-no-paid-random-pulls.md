# No paid random pulls; gameplay Familiars are earned

Every Familiar that affects gameplay is unlocked deterministically, through a Deed or a full Bond. Random pulls exist only for cosmetics and variants and are paid only with currency earned by playing; real money never buys a random outcome. We chose this, despite gacha being the usual model for the genre, because the original's progress being gated by random resources was a main thing we wanted to fix, and because paid random pulls bring odds-disclosure rules, country restrictions and a higher age rating that conflict with an everyone-friendly cozy game.

## Consequences

Any future monetisation has to be an up-front price, a one-off full-game unlock, or direct purchases of known items. The economy has a single earned currency in the personal version, built so one of those can be added later.
