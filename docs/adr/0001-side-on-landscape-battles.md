# Battles are side-on in landscape

Battles are viewed side-on in landscape, with the Keep on the left and Heroes entering from the right, as in Dungeon Squad. We chose this over a portrait top-down lane (which the earlier DS Squad prototype used) because a side-on view lets every character be drawn once, in a single three-quarter view, whereas top-down would need back views of the whole cast. The cost is one-handed play; every screen layout assumes landscape, so reversing this means redoing the UI.

## Considered Options

- Portrait, top-down lane: one-handed, but needs back views of every character.
- Portrait device with a side-on lane across the top half: keeps single-view art, but shrinks the battlefield and the Skill effects that are meant to be the spectacle.
