# Little Keep

A cute, cozy lane-defence roguelite for Android, inspired by Dungeon Squad: the Keeper and a squad of Familiars defend their home from invading Heroes. This file fixes the words we use.

## Language

### Cast

**Player**:
The person playing the game.
_Avoid_: User, gamer; never "players" for characters

**Keeper**:
The Player's own character on the battlefield, the one persistent identity across every Run.
_Avoid_: Hero, main monster, avatar, commander

**Familiar**:
A small monster that fights alongside the Keeper as part of the squad.
_Avoid_: Monster, minion, unit, hero, apostle

**Hero**:
An invading attacker; always the enemy.
_Avoid_: Enemy, mob, adventurer; never use for the Player's own characters

### Run

**Run**:
One attempt from the first Battle to the final boss or defeat.
_Avoid_: Game, session, invasion

**Chapter**:
One of the three sections of a Run, ending in a boss.
_Avoid_: Stage, world, act

**Trail**:
The side-scrolling path through a Chapter, showing only the next two steps with fog beyond and the boss as a distant landmark.
_Avoid_: Map, node map

**Battle**:
One real-time fight in a side-on lane, viewed in landscape.
_Avoid_: Fight, encounter, day

**Stop**:
One step on the Trail: a Battle, a tough Battle, a rest, a shop, a story event or a wild creature.
_Avoid_: Node, room, day

**Keep**:
The home the squad defends. Its health carries across the whole Run and the Run ends when it falls; between Runs it is the place where permanent progress lives.
_Avoid_: Base, stone, sanctum, hub

**Knock-out**:
A Keeper or Familiar being put out of a Battle for a few seconds before getting back up. Nobody on the Player's side dies.
_Avoid_: Death, kill, down

### Combat

**Skill**:
An attack or ability belonging to the Keeper or a Familiar that casts itself when ready.
_Avoid_: Special, spell, ability

**Element**:
One of four kinds of damage (fire, frost, spark, bloom), each leaving its own status on a Hero (burn, chill, shock, root).
_Avoid_: Damage type, school

**Combo**:
The burst triggered when a second Element hits a Hero already carrying another Element's status.
_Avoid_: Reaction, synergy

**Rally**:
The Keeper's one hand-triggered ability, which makes every Skill in the squad cast at once.
_Avoid_: Ultimate, panic button, pulse

### Growth within a Run

**Skill Upgrade**:
A change to one Skill, picked as 1 of 3 offered after a Battle and drawn from the squad's own skill pools.
_Avoid_: Essence, trait, perk

**Master Form**:
The evolved version of a Skill, reached after three Skill Upgrades, with its own louder look.
_Avoid_: Master skill, ultimate

**Befriending**:
A creature joining the squad as a Familiar for the rest of the Run, either freed from a defeated Hero who brought it or met as a wild creature on the Trail.
_Avoid_: Capture, captive, recruit; Heroes themselves are never befriended

### Growth across Runs

**Deed**:
A named achievement that permanently unlocks a specific Familiar.
_Avoid_: Task, quest, achievement

**Bond**:
A meter for one kind of creature that fills each time it is befriended, and at full makes it a Familiar the Player owns permanently.
_Avoid_: Souls, shards, affinity
